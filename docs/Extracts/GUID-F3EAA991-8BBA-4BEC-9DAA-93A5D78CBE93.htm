Granular Piping and Publishing (P&ID)

 
 
 

In granular form, line components are presented in flow sequence, and are broken by any break or attachment.

In an AutoCAD P&ID drawing, a schematic line is represented by a group of AcPpLineSegment entities. As these entities exist in the database, they are not flow sequenced, nor are they broken by all connections.

The granular piping components are constructed to provide a segmented description of the pipe line. They are ordered to represent flow, and granulated for every connection.

The following are AcPpGranularObject classes:

  • AcPpGranularTee
  • AcPpGranularTubing
  • AcPpGranularInlineAsset

Each granular component is derived from AcPpGranularObject. They are AcDbObjects, not drawing entities (AcDbEntity). When the utility class AcPpGranularInfo first creates them, they are memory-only objects, and are not drawing database-resident.
